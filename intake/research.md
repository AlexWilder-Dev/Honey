## The building
- Historic England list entry 1361048, "42 Silver Street including wing at rear", Grade II, listed 14 March 1975.
  Text via https://britishlistedbuildings.co.uk/101361048-42-silver-street-including-wing-at-rear-castle-ward (verified):
  "C18. Painted brick. Steep-pitched slate roof with gabled ends. Paired brackets to wide eaves. Three storeys..."
  "Including wing at rear: probably C16/17, timber framed and brick nogging ... two storeys, much altered ...
  interior, chamfered ceiling beam, arched brackets to curved tiebeam exposed in first floor room"
- Leicester City Council, Market Place Conservation Area Character Appraisal (2007),
  https://leicester.gov.uk/media/4usd0e1f/market-place-conservation-area-character-appraisal.pdf (verified):
  4.21 "part of a 15th century hall house that survives behind The Ask Restaurant at 42 Silver Street"
  4.57 "42 Silver Street (Grade II listed), an 18th century brick building, although much altered, with a classical style stone doorcase."
  4.19 "Silver Street (the first written reference to which was first made in 1587) may derive from its early occupation by
  silversmiths. It has also been known as St Martin's Churchgate ..., Hot Street (because the common bakers' oven were to be
  found there?) and 'The Street at the Backside of the Lion'! 'Shipp's Lane' or 'Sheepmarket' was another of its names although
  the sheep market was moved out to the Saturday market in 1506."
  Silver Street "follows roughly the line of the main Roman east-west street".
- Pub History Project, https://pubhistoryproject.co.uk/2026/05/05/hive-honey-42-silver-st/ : former ASK "closed post Covid";
  "Hive & Honey opened circa 2025"; "an idea from the Gaddabout Guildhall Lane and Ex Dom of Blaby team".
- Visit Leicester new openings summer 2025 (published 2025-06-02): "the team behind Dom have joined forces with Gadabout to
  create Hive & Honey on Silver Street ... in the former Ask restaurant".
